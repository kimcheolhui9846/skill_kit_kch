
## Light path
Grade the work before you start; when unsure, take the higher grade.
- L: a typo, a one-line fix, a rename, a comment. Make the change and say what changed; no task, report, or approval round.
- S: a small change in one place. Run the relevant tests and report briefly.
- C: several modules, security, data, deletion, money, or a public interface. Apply every principle and wait for approval.
